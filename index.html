Capital efficiency & monthly yield What each book earns per dollar TIED UP, which total PnL cannot tell you — the same PnL on $50 recycled weekly and on $500 parked for a month are very different businesses. locked now = cost of open positions, i.e. capital unavailable this instant. avg locked (30d) = time-weighted mean deployed capital over the trailing 30 days, not a snapshot, so a book that recycles fast shows a low average even on a heavy day. $/month = PnL from legs actually SETTLED in the last 30 days — a real monthly figure, never extrapolated from a shorter window. yield = $/month ÷ avg locked, so yield × avg locked = $/month by construction. ⚠ = fewer than 5 settlements in 30d, which is noise rather than a rate; (retired) = inventory draining, not a repeatable return.

Statistical confidence Is a book's result real, or noise? Inference unit = event (legs in one event are ONE bet — the GPT-5.6 deadline ladder, the SARB cluster — so counting legs would fake independence and shrink the interval dishonestly). Mean = return per $ deployed per event; CI = 2000-sample percentile bootstrap (robust to our heavy negative skew). "inconclusive" means we cannot yet distinguish this book from a tradeable 2% edge — not that it works. "need ~N ev" = events required to resolve it at the observed variance.
